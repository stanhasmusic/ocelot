# The economy is a Guns-spine pace track with scarce, skill-fed slack, reset every Playthrough

The coin economy implements the difficulty target set by [[0010-absolute-difficulty-curve-with-hangar-catchup]] — *buying sensibly* keeps the player at a level's expected tier, *playing well* buys ~1 tier of slack — by pacing the whole authored curve against **Guns tier as the single load-bearing axis** and treating everything else as discretionary slack. The mechanism:

- **Guns is a near-automatic pace track.** Guns = damage = the actual DPS gate, and it drives the visible `lvl_0`→`lvl_3` plane sprite, so it *is* the curve. A normal playthrough always nets enough to afford the next Guns tier each Hangar visit (3 visits, Guns 0→3). The spine never slips.
- **Scarcity and choice live in the slack** — Armour, Engine, Bombs, and gadgets. The coin supply is deliberately too small to max everything in one campaign, so the player kits *one* build identity. This is what makes the gadget loadout ([[0007-hangar-four-stat-tracks-plus-gadget-loadout]]) a real decision.
- **The skill→coin surplus is a *bounded* no-death stage bonus.** Base kill income is roughly fixed per level (spawns are authored) and funds the Guns pace track; on top sits a fixed *clear-without-dying* bonus, tuned so a clean campaign yields ~1 extra tier of slack. The **Convoy** archetype is an optional risk/reward faucet. The combo multiplier stays a **score-only** flex and never touches coins, keeping coins (progression) and score (ranking) separate per [[CONTEXT]].
- **Fixed supply, first-clear-only.** Each level pays its coins once; replaying earns nothing. Coins, owned upgrades, and progress all reset on a **New Game** — the game is a self-contained arcade arc (a **Playthrough**), not a persistent profile.
- **Escalating prices with scaling payouts.** Successive tiers cost more and later levels pay more, in lockstep; Guns stays affordable while taking a slack track to tier 3 is a genuine commitment.
- **Survival-slack vs expression-slack.** Armour/Engine are priced so a shaky-but-coin-rich player can buy ~1 tier of survivability (the coin-funded mirror of ADR-0010's skill substitution); Bombs/gadgets are where a confident player's surplus flexes. One coin pool, audience-adaptive.

We rejected several alternatives. Pacing against **per-track tier parity** or a **flat power budget** (rather than the Guns spine) either collapses the Hangar into "buy everything evenly" or lets a player buy a DPS-starved build that can't clear. A **coin combo multiplier** felt great but is unbounded — a skilled player would blow past ~1 tier and flatten the ADR-0010 sawtooth. **Farmable replay** and **persistent meta across Playthroughs (NG+)** both over-arm the player and wreck the authored curve; clean-slate keeps the supply finite and known. **Respec** was rejected to keep build choices weighty (the clean-slate reset is already the escape hatch). **Kill-volume as the skill lever** is noisy and just re-rewards survival in disguise; the no-death bonus is legible and bounded. **Flat prices** make the slack economy feel flat — escalation is the engine of build choice.

Consequence: this amends [[0005-persistent-campaign-with-hangar-metagame]] — "upgrades kept *forever*" becomes "kept for the **Playthrough**"; nothing persists across a New Game. PRD-11 (issue #39) implements two interlocked tunable curves (payout and price) that must stay in lockstep, which is why it is the first module to earn a test harness ([[0004-defer-test-framework-until-first-testable-module]]). The kill-floor funds Guns independently of the bonus, so a death-prone player keeps the spine and only forgoes *slack* — the curve's authored baseline (beatable at expected Guns tier, no slack assumed) is what protects the [[project-ocelot-target-audience]], not grinding. The prototype's `LevelSelect` screen loses its purpose (forward-only progress) and collapses to a **Continue** entry on `MainMenu`.

## Amendment — 2026-06-03 (PRD-11 economy grill)

PRD-11 (#39) solved the concrete payout/price budget and, in doing so, revised three things this ADR originally fixed. The Guns spine, the scarcity principle, the first-clear / clean-slate rules, and combo-stays-score-only all stand unchanged. The revisions:

1. **The skill→coin surplus is now two bounded faucets, not one.** The original "~1 tier of slack from a clear-without-dying bonus" understated the problem: a death-prone player — the median of the target audience, confirmed by playtest (the developer has never completed a deathless run) — earns *almost no* no-death bonus, so that bonus alone is aspirational, not progression. PRD-11 splits the clean-run slack budget (~230 coins) between **two** faucets:
   - **No-death bonus** (~120 across L1–L3, awarded per-level so partial-clean runs still bank) — the *survival* faucet.
   - **Collection** (~110 for an engaged / Coin-Magnet player) — the *engagement* faucet, earnable **even on a messy run** (you collect coins before you die). This is the long-missing mechanism behind this ADR's promise that "a shaky-but-coin-rich player can buy ~1 tier of survivability": collection is *how* a shaky player gets coin-rich. It also rescues the Coin Magnet gadget ([[0007-hangar-four-stat-tracks-plus-gadget-loadout]] / PRD-09) from economic uselessness.

   The **Convoy** is the high-variance, risk/reward spike *inside* the collection faucet — not an additive third faucet (that would over-arm) — gated by first-clear like all income. PRD-11 owns only its coin value; placement and escape mechanics belong to the level PRDs (13/14).

2. **Base income is a guaranteed clear-time floor, not collected kill income.** The original "base kill income funds Guns" was collection-dependent, so a poor collector (mobile drag-to-move, no Magnet) could under-fund the spine. PRD-11 makes base income a **floor topped up at level-complete**: collect coins during the level, and if you finish below the level's floor you are topped up to it. Missing coins now costs *slack*, never *Guns*. Coins collected *above* the floor are kept as slack (the collection faucet).

3. **Terminal coins→score bridge.** With no Hangar after the L4 finale, coins earned on L4 (and any unspent earlier) would be stranded. PRD-11 cashes unspent coins to a one-time score bonus at campaign completion — the arcade end-of-run mop-up. Coins and score stay distinct *during* play; the bridge is one-way and terminal (see [[CONTEXT]] "Coins").

The no-death / collection split (~120 / ~110) and the total slack budget are the explicit **playerbase dials** — loosenable post-playtest without touching the Guns spine. Concrete numbers live in `EconomyTunables` / `HangarTunables` / `GadgetCatalog`.
